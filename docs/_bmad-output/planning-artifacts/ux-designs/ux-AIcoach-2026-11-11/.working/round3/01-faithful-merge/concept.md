# r3-01 · Faithful Merge

**Idea:** 06's conversation, told at 09's pace. The thread, the Evidence panel and the cards stay where they were in 06. Each one now holds less.

## What came from where

- **06, The Coach Talks (structure and identity):** the palette, the wordmark and coach mark, Newsreader, Figtree and IBM Plex Mono, and the text sizes. The layout keeps 06's three parts: a left rail, the thread and the sticky Evidence panel. The coach speaks in serif. Measured numbers are mono teal with `[n]` pins. Dashed outlines mean inferred. The verdict, fault chain, practice and "Also checked" arrive as structured cards inside the thread, and a "Jump to" chip bar indexes them. Upload is a message, the composer is the drop zone, and the analysis appears as a step log in the thread. The grounding note sits under every composer.
- **09, Cairn (calm):** each card does one job. Coach messages are one or two sentences. The full explanation is hidden behind "Read my full explanation · 118 words". On the phone, the result shows the verdict, the 7%, the two frames and the feel cue. Practice is one tap away ("Next: what to practise"). The secondary pages follow 09's contact sheet: one idea per phone, with actions in the thumb zone.
- **02, Lab Instrument (figure only):** the key frames reuse 02's pose geometry: ghost body capsules, keypoint dots and connecting lines. Body lines are `#8ED3E6` on the `#36312B` ground. The hip keypoint is ringed. The impact frame overlays the dashed address pose and a "+7%" dimension arrow from the address hip line. There is no graph paper and there are no axes.

## Removed to stay simple

- Evidence panel: 06 had 6 pins and a clip timeline. Here it has **2 key frames and 1 measurement (7%)**. Pins 4–6 are folded into "3 more measurements", and the clip checks are folded into one "passed 3 of 3" row.
- The verdict card no longer repeats the key frames, since they are already in the panel. Its threshold ruler moved to the panel.
- The explanation paragraph is collapsed by default. The upload exchange collapses to one "Clip received" line.
- "Also checked" drops 06's posture ruler. Each fault gets two lines, and the possible fault stays visible.
- The checklist items lost their sub-descriptions on the phone. The landing page has six sections, each with one claim.

## Risks

- Folding the explanation and pins 4–6 behind disclosures saves space, but the "why" now depends on one tap. Players who skim may never open the 118-word explanation.
- Keeping 06's three columns still gives a busy frame at 1280 px, even with less inside. The Evidence panel probably needs to collapse into a drawer on narrow laptops, as 06 also noted.
