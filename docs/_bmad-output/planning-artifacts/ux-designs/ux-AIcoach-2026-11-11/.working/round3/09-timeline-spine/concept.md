# r3-09 · Timeline Spine

**Idea:** The swing's own timeline is how you move through the result. A slim track holds three skeleton thumbnails (address 0.86 s, top 1.80 s, impact 2.07 s), and every finding hangs on the moment it comes from.

## How it works
- **Result:** On desktop the track runs horizontally. Impact carries the measured value (7% of body height [3], rule 4%, confidence: high) beside its frame, with the dashed "likely cause" note below it. Address carries the hip line reference, and top carries "Over the top: not found". The explanation, the drill and the mobility exercise come below. "Also checked" is one quiet line.
- **Chat:** Answers link to a moment with a chip ("Impact · 2.07 s"), and the timeline highlights that moment in evidence tint. The drill card links to a moment in the same way ("Feel it at impact").
- **Analysing:** The same track fills in as the steps finish. Pose draws the bodies, swing events pin the times, measurements attach to their moments, then the rule table and explanation follow. Before upload the spine is three dashed placeholders.
- **Phone:** The spine turns vertical down the left edge, with notes to the right. The chat sheet keeps a mini horizontal timeline above it.
- **Honest states:** For "Cannot analyse", the spine is dashed and empty, and each reason hangs where the clip broke (whole clip, address, impact) with a link to the filming guide step. For Nelly's swing, the spine is calm and the only note is a dashed, low-confidence unverified observation at the top. In the swing library, every row is a tiny three-dot spine with the finding moment in teal.

## Risks
- Top and impact are only 0.27 s apart, so the track is spaced evenly rather than to scale. The times are shown in mono, but the spacing is not proportional.
- Findings that span moments (loss of posture compares downswing with address) do not fit one node cleanly. They fall back to the "Also checked" line.
