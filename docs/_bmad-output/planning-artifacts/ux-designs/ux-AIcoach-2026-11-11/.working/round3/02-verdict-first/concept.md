# R3-02 · Verdict First

**Idea:** The result opens on one calm verdict screen: fault name, a one-line feel cue, confidence and the impact key frame drawn large. There are exactly two ways on, "See why" and "What to practise". The coach conversation is a second layer that opens beside the verdict (desktop) or as a sheet under it (phone), only when asked.

This answers the risk in 06 that chat-as-frame is slow. Rory gets the answer in one glance, and the conversation is still one tap away.

## Taken from 06 / 09 / 02
- **06:** the whole visual language (palette, coach mark and *AI*coach wordmark, Newsreader / Figtree / IBM Plex Mono, text sizes). The coach speaks in serif with its mark. Measured values are mono teal, with pin [1] linking the "+7%" on the frame to its caption and the "Based on" footnote. Dashed means inferred: the likely-cause node, the unverified observation and the verdict slot that is still waiting. The grounded-chat lock note is kept. Upload stays a coach message: the checklist is its message and the composer is the drop zone. The analysing state streams in as a step log.
- **09:** one idea per screen, actions in the thumb zone on the phone, short copy, and progressive disclosure (verdict → why → practise → ask). The secondary pages are single-purpose phone frames. Every outcome uses the same verdict shape: label, one name, one line, at most two actions. That includes Cannot analyse and No verified fault found.
- **02:** the pose skeleton (keypoint dots and connecting lines, built from 02's coordinates), restyled as 06 body lines `#8ED3E6` on key-frame ground `#36312B`. Faint body capsules stay behind it. The impact frame overlays the dashed address pose and a "+7%" dimension from the address backside line.

## Removed to stay simple
- 06's left conversation rail, its sticky Evidence panel, its "Jump to" chip bar, and the long thread of structured cards on the result page.
- The fault chain, the possible fault and the clip checks are not on the first screen. They live under "See why" (the verdict carries only a one-line "Also checked").
- The landing page has no fault table. Three small cards replace it.

## Risks
- With two buttons and the chat hidden, some players may never open "See why" and so miss the likely cause and "Loss of posture: possible". The one-line "Also checked" has to stay on the verdict.
- The chat is now secondary, so the grounded coach may feel less like the product. It is reached only through the two layers, and on the phone only from the sheet.
