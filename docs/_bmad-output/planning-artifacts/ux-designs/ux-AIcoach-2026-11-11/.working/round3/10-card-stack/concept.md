# 10 · Coach Card Stack

**Idea:** The coach answers in a short stack of large, calm cards in one column. Each card holds one idea (What I saw, Why it happens, This week's practice, Ask me), opens with the coach's voice in Newsreader, and folds to a single line when you are done with it.

It sits between 06's thread and 09's one-screen-per-step. It has the order and the voice of a conversation, but each part of the answer stays in a fixed place.

## What I took, and how

- **From 06 (main design):** the palette, wordmark and coach mark, the Newsreader, Figtree and IBM Plex Mono roles, and 06's text sizes. The card surface is 06's: the card colour, a hairline, and a faint offset shadow. Measured values are mono teal with numbered pins (1–2 key frames, 3–4 measurements). Dashed means inferred and solid means measured. The landing keeps 06's hero, its "rules" band, the fault table, the "says no" trio and the dark call-to-action band. The grounded chat keeps its lock note and "Based on" line.
- **From 09:** one idea per card, progressive disclosure, short copy, and every phone action in the bottom third. The other-pages sheet is a set of one-idea phone frames. Confirmations open inside the card, never in a pop-up.
- **From 02:** only the figure. It is the pose skeleton with keypoint dots and lines, using 02's address and impact coordinates. Body lines are `#8ED3E6` on the `#36312B` key-frame ground, with the body mass toned into the ground. The hip keypoint is emphasised, and a dashed line marks where the hips were at address. On the impact frame, a `+7%` arrow shows how far they moved.

## The card grammar (used on every page)

- **Collapsed:** mark, card title (Figtree), a one-line summary in Newsreader, and a chevron.
- **Expanded:** the same header, then a Newsreader opener, then the content.
- **Ghost (dashed):** a card the coach has not earned yet. While the clip is analysed, Why, Practice and Ask wait as dashed outlines and fill in as the steps finish.
- The filming guide, library, rejection, no-fault and account pages are the same stack. The filming steps, the saved swings and the account sections are all folded cards that open in place.

## What I removed to stay simple

06's left conversation rail, the sticky Evidence panel, the "Jump to" chips, the clip-checks list and the fault-chain boxes (now a vertical node chain with no nested boxes). The possible fault and over the top are two quiet lines under "Also checked", and the second figure overlay is gone (only the hip line remains). The desktop result is one 760 px column with empty margins.

## Risks

- Folded cards hide content. A player who never opens "Why it happens" misses the cause. The one-line summaries have to carry the gist, and the first card always arrives open.
- Four similar cards can feel like a form. The serif opener in each card and the strict one-idea rule are what keep the stack conversational. If either slips, it turns into a settings page.
