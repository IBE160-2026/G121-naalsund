# r3-04 · Skeleton Hero

**Idea:** The pose figure carries the page. Your own skeleton is the result, and every measurement sits on the keypoint it was measured from.

## What I took, and how

- **From 06 (main language):** all of the palette, the mark and wordmark, Newsreader for the coach's voice, Figtree for the UI, and IBM Plex Mono only for measured values. Teal numbered pins, dashed outlines for inferred items, the coach tag, the clay CTA and the grounded-chat lock note all come from 06 too. The landing headline is the 06 headline, and the upload page keeps 06's "coach asks for one clip" wording and its step log.
- **From 09:** one idea per section and per phone screen, short copy, and progressive disclosure ("Read the full explanation", "Also checked" folded into two quiet rows). Actions sit low on the phone in thumb reach. The result runs Fault, Why, Practise, Ask, the same order as 09's stepper.
- **From 02:** the figure construction, rebuilt from 02's key-frame keypoint coordinates (head, neck, shoulder, elbow, hands, hip, knee, ankle, toe, heel, club, ball, in body-height units). It is restyled for 06: key-frame ground `#36312B`, body lines `#8ED3E6`, a faint body mass in `#4A433A`, the address pose as a dashed ghost, and a clean dimension line for the 7% hip shift.

## Signature moves

1. **Landing hero = a film strip.** Three frames (address, top, impact) share one dashed address-hip line, so you can see the hip leave the line at impact (`7%` [3]).
2. **Result hero = one big figure.** The impact skeleton sits over the faint address skeleton with the hip shift as a dimension line. Evidence pins are small cards attached to keypoints: [3] hips toward the ball, [4] lead hip turn, [5] posture angle. Pins [1] and [2] are the two frames. A timeline scrubber (address, top, impact) runs along the floor of the frame. The coach's words sit beside the figure.
3. **Analysing = the figure draws itself.** Keypoints appear, bones join, and the dimension line shows as "measuring…" until its pin lands.
4. **The chat points back at the figure.** A small copy of the figure sits beside the conversation. The pins the answer cites are lit, and the rest are outlined.
5. **The same figure tells the honest outcomes.** Cannot analyse shows feet cut off by the frame edge. No verified fault shows Nelly's hips staying on the line with no pins.

## Removed to stay simple

06's left conversation rail and its sticky Evidence panel (the pins now live on the figure). The "Jump to" chip bar became four quiet tabs. The fault chain lost its prose, and the explanation sits behind a disclosure link. Posture and Over the top are one line each. 02's graph paper, axis ticks, IDs and error bars are gone, and so are 09's cairn stones.

## Risks

- Pin cards on the figure can cover joints on narrow screens. The phone uses bare numbered pins (tap to open), so that interaction has to be built and tested.
- A large, exact-looking skeleton can suggest more precision than 2D pose delivers. The confidence words and the dashed "inferred" convention have to stay visible next to every pin.
