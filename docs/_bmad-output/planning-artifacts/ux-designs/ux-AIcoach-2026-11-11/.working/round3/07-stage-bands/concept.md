# 07 · Stage Bands

**Idea:** The page has two kinds of full-width band. When it shows the swing, the band is dark (06's key-frame ground `#36312B` with `#8ED3E6` skeleton lines). When the coach talks or you act, the band is light paper. Each band holds one idea.

## How the rhythm works

- **Landing:** the bands tell the story. Paper hero, then a dark band (1 · Film: phone on a stand, golfer at address), paper (2 · Upload: one card, one button), dark (3 · Answer: what it measured, with the address and impact key frames and 7%), paper (3 · Answer: what the coach says, with the feel cue and a short "ask why" exchange), dark (the three faults, one figure each), paper (honest answers and the grounded-chat refusal), and a quiet paper-2 call to action at the end.
- **Upload:** the coach's request sits on paper. A dark band follows with the six filming essentials drawn onto the set-up (hand height line, 2.4–3.7 m, frame brackets, and pills for 120 fps and one swing). The drop zone is on paper. While it analyses, the dark band shows address, top and impact frames as their body lines are drawn, on a timeline. The five steps tick off on paper.
- **Result:** the verdict is on paper. Then one dark band holds the key frames and the single measurement that matters (7% against the 4% rule, confidence high). After that everything is paper: why it happens (feel cue, explanation, vertical fault chain), what to practise, a collapsed "Also checked" section, and the chat. The chat shows the cited impact frame as a small dark tile.
- **Phone:** each screen opens on a dark swing band and ends on paper, with one action in thumb reach.

## Taken from

- **06:** palette, Newsreader/Figtree/IBM Plex Mono and their sizes, the mark and wordmark, coach label, measured values in mono teal with `[n]` pins, dashed = inferred, the grounded-chat note and "Based on" footnote, and the clean drop-zone layout from 02-upload.
- **09:** one idea per band or screen, short copy, big thumb-zone buttons, progressive disclosure (only the 7% is on the result's stage band, and "Also checked" is collapsed to one line per fault).
- **02:** the pose model. Keypoint dots and connecting lines use 02's exact address and impact coordinates, with faint body capsules (line colour at 11%). On impact, the dashed address ghost and the hip-gap arrow are kept. They are restyled as `#8ED3E6` on `#36312B`, with no graph paper.

## Removed to stay simple

06's three-column shell (rail, thread, sticky evidence panel). The 31°, 6° and hand-path measurements are no longer on the result's stage; they live in the chain and in "Also checked". The "Jump to" chips, the clip-check list on the result, the separate swing-events log and the drill pictograms are gone. Only body movement is drawn, and always on dark.

## Risks

- A long run of full-width dark bands can feel heavy or cinematic on a phone in sunlight at the range. The bands must stay short, and paper must always carry the action.
- The rule "dark = swing" only works if it is strict. One dark band used for decoration (for example, a dark call to action) would break the meaning, and the swing-library thumbnails stretch it a little.
