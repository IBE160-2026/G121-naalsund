# r3-03 · One Thing Per Screen

**Idea:** The result is four calm steps (Fault, Why, Practise, Ask the coach). Each step is its own screen, the coach's sentence opens it, and the chat is one tap away from every step.

## What came from where

- **06 (skin):** the full palette, Newsreader / Figtree / IBM Plex Mono at 06's sizes, the flag-mark and *AI*coach wordmark, the clay coach label, mono-teal measured values with numbered evidence pins (`[2] impact frame`, `[3] hip movement`), dashed outline only for the inferred likely cause and the unverified observation, and the grounded-chat note under the composer. The landing keeps 06's hero line and its honesty section.
- **09 (structure):** the most 09-like part of round 3. Phone first, one main thing per screen, a step bar at the top, the primary action in the thumb zone, and "Also checked" moved into a sheet instead of shown up front. Upload is four phones: film, ready, analysing, then "your answer is ready" as a short contents page of the four steps.
- **02 (figure):** the key frames use 02's skeleton construction (keypoint dots joined by lines, faint body capsules behind), recoloured to `#8ED3E6` on `#36312B`. The hip keypoint is ringed because it is the measured point. The drill illustration uses the same skeleton in ink on paper.

## Step rules
- Every step opens with `Coach` plus one serif sentence. On Why, the feel cue is that sentence.
- "Ask the coach about this" sits under every step. It opens step 4 with that step as context. The thread itself only lives in step 4.
- The step bar adapts to the result: Nelly's no-fault result has only **Result · Ask**, and a rejection has no step bar.
- On desktop, the steps form one vertical stepper. One step is open, and the others fold to their coach sentence. Once a step is done, it folds to a one-line summary of facts.

## Removed to stay simple
06's left conversation rail, the persistent Evidence panel, the "Jump to" chip bar, the swing-event timeline and the clip checks. The full explanation sits behind "Read my full explanation". The possible fault, the fault not found and the save toggle are in the "Also checked" sheet. The upload drop zone has a solid outline so that dashed lines keep meaning only "inferred".

## Risks
- Four taps to reach the chat can feel slow for returning players who only want the verdict. The contents page and "ask straight away" link help, but a compact summary view may be needed.
- With the Evidence panel gone, the pins on later steps point back to step 1 instead of staying visible. That keeps the screens calm but asks the player to remember or go back.
