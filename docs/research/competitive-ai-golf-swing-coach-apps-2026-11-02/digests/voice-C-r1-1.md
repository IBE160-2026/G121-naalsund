## Cross-cutting synthesis (evidence-weighted)
- **Strongest wedge: interpretation burden.** Users see faults or numbers but don't know what to do or where the fault starts (#2, #6, #8, #10). Sportsbox draws "overwhelming"/"not beginner friendly"; Mustard draws "YOU have to figure out where". Where an app *does* explain causes with visual evidence, that is exactly what reviewers praise (#5).
- **Trust depends on camera setup.** Small changes in setup change the outputs (#1, #7). Users praise strict filming guidelines (#10). Showing your evidence (key frames, drawn lines) builds confidence (#5, #10).
- **Price and paywall resentment** centres on monthly fees of about $24-30 and paywalls before any value is shown (#3, #8, #13).
- **AI distrust** is present among forum golfers (generic or wrong answers, ingraining faults), but it is moderate, not hostile (#10). Users with a coach want the AI to agree with that coach (#11).
