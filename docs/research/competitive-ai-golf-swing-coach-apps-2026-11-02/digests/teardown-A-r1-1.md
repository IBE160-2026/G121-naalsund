## Gaps between claims and product

- Sportsbox's "#1 on Tour" and "Tour-trusted" lead the homepage, but the cheapest paid tier ($110/yr) exposes only 6 measurements. The full 3D data costs $400/yr, so the core 3D promise is effectively gated.
- SAMI was announced as "agentic" and "prescriptive", but the App Store describes "preset questions and personalized guidance" and labels it beta (Sep 2026). Depth is unverified.
- HackMotion's "virtual coaching" only covers the wrist (a single body segment). It cannot see hips, pelvis or posture faults.

## Leads worth chasing

- Sportsbox help center (Zendesk or knowledge base): exact capture requirements (DTL vs face-on, tripod), accuracy and validation studies, and whether metrics carry confidence.
- Sportsbox "Markerless 3D Motion Analysis" blog post, and any published validation against marker-based mocap.
- Hands-on SAMI review (golf.com, MyGolfSpy, GolfWRX, YouTube) to test whether it reasons causally or only paraphrases metrics.
- Sportsbox pricing on web.archive.org (late 2025) to detect a price change after the acquisition. Not done because of budget.
- The HackMotion Google Play or App Store changelog (the Apple URL used returned 404) for 2025–26 releases, and a HackMotion "Swing Report" or video sync feature.
- HackMotion's current list price for the Pro tier with Sensor 4.
- MyGolfSpy or Golf Digest independent tests of both products.

## Looked for, not found

- Sportsbox: published accuracy numbers or per-metric confidence; explicit fault-to-fault cause-effect explanations; mobility or fitness exercises; official capture-setup docs; a price change history.
- HackMotion: any AI, LLM or chat feature or 2026 AI announcement; accuracy specs; list prices on the pricing page; a coach dashboard or multi-student portal; any body-segment data beyond the wrist; mobility or fitness content.
