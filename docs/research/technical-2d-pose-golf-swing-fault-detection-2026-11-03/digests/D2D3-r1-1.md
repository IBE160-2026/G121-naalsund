GolfDB contains 1,400 golf swing videos labelled with 8 event frames, bounding box, player name/sex, club type and view type (face-on, down-the-line, other). | https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.06528 ; https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1903.06528 | arXiv / CVPR Workshops (CVSports) 2019 | 2019-03 | accessed 2026-11-03 | high | event-detection
2. GolfDB videos were sampled from YouTube at 30 fps, 720p; real-time and slow-motion clips contribute roughly equal frame totals (~195k each). Counts per view (DTL vs face-on) are not reported, nor accuracy per view. | https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1903.06528 | arXiv | 2019-03 | accessed 2026-11-03 | high | event-detection
3. PCE (Percentage of Correct Events) uses tolerance δ = 1 frame for real-time 30 fps video; for slow-motion δ = max(round(n/f),1) where n = frames Address→Impact, f = sampling frequency. I.e. at real-time, correct = within ±1 frame at 30 fps (~±33 ms). | https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1903.06528 | arXiv | 2019-03 | accessed 2026-11-03 | high | event-detection
4. SwingNet-160 per-event PCE (overall / slow-mo / real-time): Address 31.7/23.5/38.7; Toe-up 84.2/80.7/87.2; Mid-backswing 88.7/84.7/92.1; Top 83.9/75.7/90.8; Mid-downswing 98.1/97.8/98.3; Impact 98.4/98.3/98.4; Mid-follow-through 97.6/98.0/97.2; Finish 26.5/21.5/30.7; overall 76.1/72.5/79.2. Six events excl. Address/Finish = 91.8%. | https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1903.06528 | arXiv | 2019-03 | accessed 2026-11-03 | high | event-detection
5. SwingNet is MobileNetV2 + recurrent (hybrid CNN-RNN); input 160×160, train sequence length 64; CPU time 10.6 ms/frame (T=64) on Intel i7-8700K excluding I/O (10.8 ms at T=32, 13.8 ms at T=4). CPU-feasible offline. | https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1903.06528 ; https://github.com/wmcnally/golfdb | arXiv / GitHub | 2019-03 | accessed 2026-11-03 | high | event-detection

10. Downswing duration: TPI 3D range 233–296 ms; rule of thumb fast swing 750 ms back / 250 ms down, slow 900/300; backswing:downswing ≈3:1 (TPI tour driver ≈3.2). At 30 fps clubhead at ~100 mph moves ~5 ft between frames; at 240 fps ~7.5 in. Recommends ≥120 fps (240 suggested) and shutter ≥1/1000 s; 30 fps gives too little info around impact. → A 250–300 ms downswing yields only ~8–9 frames at 30 fps, ~15–18 at 60, ~30–36 at 120, ~60–72 at 240 (arithmetic from these numbers). | https://cdn.site.mytpi.com/articles/biomechanics/measuring-the-timing-of-the-golf-swing-from-video?search=ART | Titleist Performance Institute (Phil Cheetham) | unknown | accessed 2026-11-03 | medium | capture
